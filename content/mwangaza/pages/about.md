### Our Story
For decades, the villages of Miriu Sub Location have lived in the shadow of the Sondu Miriu Dam — a source of electricity for the rest of Kenya, but not for the communities who live beside it. Without power, without clean water, with roads swallowed by hippo grass and wild animals ranging freely along the river, these communities have struggled to access the most basic rights.

Community members began fighting for change as early as 2000, physically blocking roads and confronting KENGEN during protests that culminated in demonstrations in 2018. One person died. Injuries were many. But nothing changed.

In 2020, the community connected with AfriNov — the African Centre for Non-Violence — which introduced a new path: organised, peaceful, evidence-based advocacy. With AfriNov's support, the community formed a legal entity so its voice could be heard in formal spaces.

On 27 November 2020, Mwangaza Abunya Community Development Group was officially registered as a Community-Based Organisation. The name — Mwangaza, meaning "light" — was chosen deliberately: to bring light to a community left in darkness by the very dam that generates power for others.

In 2021 and 2022, AfriNov equipped members with skills in governance, financial literacy, Turning the Tides methodology, and climate policy awareness. That training led directly to Petition No. 002 of 2022, filed with the National Assembly. In May 2022, Mwangaza leaders travelled to Nairobi and successfully defended the petition before Parliament. The report was adopted for implementation.

Today, Mwangaza is a member of the Homa Bay County Civil Societies Organisations Network — no longer a ward-level group, but a recognised civil society voice for marginalised communities across Rachuonyo East Sub-County. In July 2025, the organisation upgraded its governance structure with a new Board of Management to match its expanded mandate.

The journey from 2000 to today is proof that communities, when organised and supported, can change the terms of their own story.

### Mission
"To develop efficient and healthy communities through sustainable food security and livelihoods, innovations, capacity building, sensitisation, and advocating for the rights of society through effective networking."

### Vision
"An improved, empowered, and transformed society. Communities where every person has access to food security, quality health care, and education — building a foundation for lasting dignity and growth."

### Core Values (from constitution)
- Integrity and ethical conduct
- Transparency and accountability
- Community-centred decision making
- Peaceful, evidence-based advocacy
- Gender equality and inclusion

### Objectives
1. Promote sustainable agricultural practices to improve livelihoods and food security
2. Advocate for environmental conservation and climate change adaptation
3. Provide community education and awareness on health and social issues
4. Lobby and advocate for human rights, including land rights and access to utilities
5. Mainstream gender equality, STI and HIV/AIDS awareness
6. Promote partnerships and effective networking with relevant organisations

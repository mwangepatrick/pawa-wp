### Our Story
Communities along the Sondu Miriu Dam have advocated for their rights since 2000. After years of peaceful protests that culminated in 2018, the community connected with AfriNov in 2020. Mwangaza was officially registered in 2021 — its name meaning "light" — a beacon for those left behind by development projects.

In 2022, the organisation filed Petition No. 002 of 2022 and successfully defended it before Parliament, marking a milestone in community advocacy.

### Mission
"To develop efficient and healthy communities through sustainable food security and livelihoods, innovations, capacity building, sensitisation, and advocating for the rights of society through effective networking."

### Vision
"An improved, empowered, and transformed society. Communities where every person has access to food security, quality health care, and education — building a foundation for lasting dignity and growth."

### Objectives
1. Promote sustainable agricultural practices
2. Advocate for environmental conservation and climate adaptation
3. Provide health and social education
4. Lobby for human rights protections
5. Mainstream gender equality and health awareness
6. Build partnerships with relevant organisations

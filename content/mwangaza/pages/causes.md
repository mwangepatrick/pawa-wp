## Introduction
Since the inception of the Sondu Miriu Dam, the communities of Kabondo East Ward have been denied the basic rights that every Kenyan deserves. Mwangaza fights for these causes — peacefully, persistently, and with evidence.

In 2022 we took our fight to Parliament. The National Assembly adopted our recommendations. Now we are holding decision-makers accountable to implement them.

## Cause 1: Electricity for the Sondu Miriu Communities
**Status:** Unimplemented  
The Sondu Miriu Hydroelectric Dam generates electricity — but the villages beside it have none. Households spend KSH 200+ to photocopy a single page because there are no nearby services. Students were locked out of online learning during COVID because they had to walk over 10 km to find internet access.

**Our ask:** Connection to the National Grid via KENGEN, Last Mile Connectivity, or REREC.

---

## Cause 2: Clean, Safe and Adequate Water
**Status:** Unimplemented  
The river is the only water source — yet heavy sedimentation and hippo grass blockage make it inaccessible. Communities rely on dug-out water points. As of 2025, 14 families in the area are under medical treatment for bilharzia, including fathers, mothers and children.

**Our ask:** Provision of clean, safe and adequate water points for domestic use.

---

## Cause 3: Removal of Hippo Grass (Water Weeds)
**Status:** Stalled — cleared only 1km before stopping  
Invasive hippo grass has blocked access to the river bank for both humans and livestock. It obstructs the major road between Kisumu and Homa Bay counties. It creates stagnant water breeding malaria and waterborne disease.

**Our ask:** Full removal of water weeds along the Sondu Miriu Dam banks.

---

## Cause 4: Access Roads Along the River Bank
**Status:** Identified but not actioned  
Identification of roads to be opened was done — but no construction has followed. The main access road to Rachuonyo East Sub-County Hospital remains blocked.

**Our ask:** Opening and grading of access roads along the dam.

---

## Cause 5: Mamboleo Health Centre
**Status:** Funds allocated (2025) — construction pending  
There is no functioning health facility within the immediate community. The nearest dispensary requires long-distance travel. KENGEN has allocated funds to build a health facility at Mamboleo Grounds — Mwangaza is monitoring this commitment.

**Our ask:** Construction and operationalisation of Mamboleo Health Centre.

---

## Cause 6: Bridge at Wath Karoko
**Status:** Funds allocated (2025) — construction pending  
A bridge is critical for access across the river. KENGEN has allocated funds for the Wath Karoko bridge. Mwangaza is monitoring this commitment.

**Our ask:** Construction of a fully load-bearing bridge at Wath Karoko.

---

## Cause 7: CSR School Construction (Abuoye & Nyandolo Schools)
**Status:** Stalled — skeletal structures abandoned  
KENGEN began constructing classrooms at Abuoye and Nyandolo Comprehensive Schools under CSR commitments. Two years later, skeleton structures stand abandoned. Learners are without classrooms.

**Our ask:** Completion of the school buildings as committed by KENGEN.

---

## Cause 8: Water Points for Livestock
**Status:** Unimplemented  
The hippo grass blockage denies livestock access to the river. Dedicated water points for domestic animals along the river bank are urgently needed.

**Our ask:** Provision of water points for livestock along the Sondu Miriu bank.

---

## Cause 9: Title Deeds
**Status:** Unimplemented  
Community members along the dam area have faced long delays in receiving title deeds to their land.

**Our ask:** Expedited processing and issuance of title deeds for affected households.

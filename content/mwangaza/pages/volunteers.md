## Page Intro
Mwangaza believes that change is built by people who show up. We welcome volunteers who want to contribute their time, skills, and energy to transforming communities in Kabondo East Ward and Rachuonyo East Sub-County.

Whether you are a local community member, a student, a professional, or an organisation — there is a place for you in this work.

## Volunteer Areas

### Community Health & Awareness
Support health education sessions, HIV/AIDS awareness campaigns, drug prevention programmes, and nutrition outreach. Basic health or community development knowledge is a plus but not required.

### Environmental Conservation
Join tree planting drives, school woodlot establishment (Learn and Grow Project), and national environmental day mobilisation. Physical fitness and community spirit required.

### Agriculture & Food Security
Assist with the Go Green Initiative farming operations along the Sondu Miriu River — planting, irrigation, and harvest support for the vegetable production project.

### Advocacy & Documentation
Help document community issues, prepare reports, and support petition follow-up. Writing, photography, or legal skills are especially welcome.

### Youth Engagement
Work directly with young people on drug prevention, education support, and community leadership. Passion for youth development essential.

### Administrative & Communications
Support the secretariat with meeting documentation, communication, and organisational records. Computer literacy helpful.

## Requirements (from CBO Constitution)
- Must be at least 18 years of age
- Must be committed to the CBO's objectives
- Must abide by Mwangaza's code of conduct
- Volunteer policy in development — will be adopted by the Board of Management

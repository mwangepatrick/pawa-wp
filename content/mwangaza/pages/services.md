## Programmes

### 1. Climate Action & Environmental Conservation

#### Go Green Live Longer Initiative
Establishing green spaces in institutions and public places across Rachuonyo East Sub-County. A notable achievement is the design of the roundabout at the DCC's roundabout in Ramula town centre.

#### Learn and Grow Project
Working with Environmental Clubs in schools to establish woodlots and plant trees, promoting vegetation cover in the region. Ongoing collaboration with students builds long-term environmental stewardship in the community.

#### National Tree Planting & Environmental Days
Mwangaza participates in Kenya's national tree planting days alongside other partner organisations to enhance forest cover. Members mobilise community participation during these events.

### 2. Food Security — Go Green Initiative

Mwangaza runs an active horticultural farming project on approximately **5 acres** of land along the Sondu Miriu River in Abuoye Village, Miriu Sub Location. Crops include:
- Kales
- Cabbages
- Black nightshade
- Cow peas

**Challenge:** Drought causes heavy losses even near the river because the hippo grass barrier blocks direct access to the water. Mwangaza currently uses a water pump to irrigate, raising costs and making production unsustainable.

**Ongoing ask:** Solar irrigation kit to replace diesel pump — request made to partners to fund the equipment to secure year-round production.

### 4. Human Rights Advocacy — Sondu Miriu Campaign

The founding purpose of Mwangaza. For over two decades the communities along the Sondu Miriu Dam have faced:
- No electricity despite generating power within the locality
- No clean water — heavy sedimentation in the river
- Blocked river access due to hippo grass (affecting both people and livestock)
- Dangerous wild animals in riverside areas
- Blocked access roads to Rachuonyo East Sub-County Hospital
- Incomplete CSR projects by KENGEN (school classrooms stalled, hippo grass clearing stalled)
- Overstayed title deed applications

**Petition No. 002 of 2022** — filed with the National Assembly; successfully defended in Parliament in May 2022. Report adopted for implementation. Most recommendations had a 90-day timeline; as of 2025 many remain unimplemented or stalled. Mwangaza continues to press KENGEN and government stakeholders.

**Active advocacy milestones (2025):**
- April 2025: Organised stakeholder forum with KENGEN at Rachuonyo East Sub-County HQ
- December 2025: Addressed Cabinet Secretary for Energy Hon. James Opiyo Wandayi during his Kabondo Kasipul visit; called for electricity connection and full implementation of petition recommendations
- KENGEN has allocated funds for a health facility at Mamboleo Grounds and a bridge at Wath Karoko — Mwangaza monitoring progress

### 6. Economic Empowerment

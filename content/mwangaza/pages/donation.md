## Page Intro
Your donation supports communities in Kabondo East Ward, Homa Bay County, who have been fighting for over two decades to secure their basic rights — electricity, clean water, safe roads, and education.

Every contribution directly funds:
- Advocacy campaigns and legal petition follow-up
- Community health education
- Food security farming projects
- Youth drug-prevention programmes
- Environmental conservation work

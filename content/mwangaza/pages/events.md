### Drug-Free Society Campaign
**Date:** 5 September 2025 (completed)
**Venue:** Community hall, Abuoye area
**Description:** A community education session targeting youth and young couples on the dangers of alcohol, bhang, and tobacco use. High rates of school dropouts, teenage pregnancies, and crime in the area are linked to substance abuse. The event brought together 25+ participants for a half-day awareness campaign.

**Date:** 30 April 2025 (completed)
**Venue:** Rachuonyo East Sub-County HQ, DCC Boardroom, Ramula — 9am
**Description:** Face-to-face meeting between Mwangaza and KENGEN to review the stalled implementation of Parliament's recommendations from Petition No. 002 of 2022. Key stakeholders were invited to agree on a roadmap for implementation.

### AGM — Constitutional Reforms & Governance Upgrade
**Date:** 25 July 2025 (completed)
**Venue:** TBD (notified by Secretary Ondanya Barack)
**Description:** Annual General Meeting to review and reform the constitution, upgrade from SHG to full CBO governance model, establish a new Board of Management, and elect interim board members.
**Outcome:** Interim Board of Management established; full board inauguration targeted for 18 August 2025; constitution formally adopted 29 September 2025

### Constitution Adoption Meeting
**Date:** 29 September 2025 (completed)
**Venue:** Abuoye Primary School (3pm)
**Description:** Formal meeting to adopt the new CBO constitution, confirm office bearers, and establish bank account by-laws. All signatories signed the constitution.
**Outcome:** Constitution adopted; Equity Bank (Oyugis) account confirmed with three signatories.

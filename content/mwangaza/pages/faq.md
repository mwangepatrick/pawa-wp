## Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: What is Mwangaza Integrated Project?**
Mwangaza Integrated Project is a Community-Based Organisation (CBO) registered in Homa Bay County, Kenya. We work in Kabondo East Ward, Rachuonyo East Sub-County, fighting for the rights of communities marginalised by the Sondu Miriu Dam — and running programmes in food security, health, environmental conservation, and economic empowerment.

---

**Q: What does "Mwangaza" mean?**
Mwangaza means "light" in Luo and Swahili. The name was chosen deliberately — to bring light to communities left in darkness by a dam that generates electricity for others but not for those who live beside it.

---

**Q: When was Mwangaza founded?**
Community advocacy along the Sondu Miriu Dam began in 2000. Mwangaza was formally registered as a Community-Based Organisation on 27 November 2020, and upgraded its governance structure to a full CBO Board of Management in 2025.

---

**Q: What is the Sondu Miriu Petition?**
In 2022, Mwangaza filed Petition No. 002 of 2022 with the National Assembly of Kenya, documenting the injustices faced by communities along the Sondu Miriu Dam — including no electricity, no clean water, blocked roads, dangerous hippo grass, and stalled KENGEN CSR projects. The National Assembly adopted the petition report on 26 May 2022. Mwangaza continues to push for full implementation.

---

**Q: Where does Mwangaza operate?**
Our primary area is Kabondo East Ward, Rachuonyo East Sub-County, Homa Bay County — specifically Abuoye Village, Miriu Sub Location, along the Sondu Miriu River. We also work in Kabondo West Ward and advocate at county level as a member of the Homa Bay County Civil Societies Organisations Network.

**Q: How is Mwangaza funded?**
Mwangaza is funded through membership fees, contributions, fundraising activities, farm produce sales, donations from well-wishers, and donor grants. Financial accounts are held at Equity Bank, Oyugis, with three signatories required for all withdrawals.

---

**Q: How do I become a member?**
Membership is open to any Kenyan citizen aged 18 or over who is committed to Mwangaza's objectives. Members must pay a registration fee of KSH 500 and an annual fee of KSH 1,000. Applications are approved by the Management Committee. Contact us for details.

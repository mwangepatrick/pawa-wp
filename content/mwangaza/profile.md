# Mwangaza Integrated Project — Organisation Profile

## Identity
- **Full name:** Mwangaza Abunya Community Development Group
- **Also known as:** Mwangaza Integrated Project
- **Meaning:** "Mwangaza" = "light" (Luo/Swahili)
- **Type:** Community-Based Organisation (CBO)
- **Location:** Kabondo East Ward, Rachuonyo East Sub-County, Homa Bay County, Kenya
- **P.O. Box:** 13440-223, Kadongo, Homa Bay County
- **Registration year:** 2021 (community advocacy since 2000)

## History
- Communities along the Sondu Miriu Dam have advocated for their rights since 2000
- After unsuccessful protests culminating in 2018, the community engaged with AfriNov in 2020
- Officially registered as a CBO in 2021
- Filed Petition No. 002 of 2022; successfully defended before Parliament in May 2022

## Mission
"To develop efficient and healthy communities through sustainable food security and livelihoods, innovations, capacity building, sensitisation, and advocating for the rights of society through effective networking."

## Vision
"An improved, empowered, and transformed society. Communities where every person has access to food security, quality health care, and education — building a foundation for lasting dignity and growth."

# Brand Color Palette — KISWA Kenya

## Recommended Palette

Based on the current site design (purple hero, amber accents, magenta CTAs) and KISWA's identity as a feminist women's empowerment organisation in Kisumu, Kenya.

---

### Primary — Deep Purple
**`#7E22CE`**
- Use for: hero backgrounds, section headers, primary buttons, nav bar
- Meaning: dignity, women's empowerment, rights — purple is the international colour of women's suffrage and activism

---

### Secondary — Magenta / Hot Pink
**`#EC4899`**
- Use for: CTA buttons (Donate, Get Involved), highlights, tags, hover states
- Meaning: bold activism, visibility, energy, boldness in advocacy

---

### Accent — Amber / Golden Yellow
**`#F59E0B`**
- Use for: bullet points, icon accents, stat counters, secondary buttons, alert banners
- Meaning: hope, Kenyan warmth, sunlight, community spirit

---

### Background Light
**`#F5F3FF`** (Lavender tint)
- Use for: section alternating backgrounds, card backgrounds
- Pairs naturally with the primary purple

---

### Dark / Text
**`#1E1B4B`** (Deep Indigo)
- Use for: body text, headings on white, footer background
- Provides depth without being harsh black

---

### White / Clean
**`#FFFFFF`**
- Use for: main content backgrounds, text on dark sections

---

## Colour Roles Summary

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Purple | `#7E22CE` |
| Secondary | Magenta | `#EC4899` |
| Accent | Amber | `#F59E0B` |
| Background | Lavender Tint | `#F5F3FF` |
| Text / Dark | Deep Indigo | `#1E1B4B` |
| Clean | White | `#FFFFFF` |

---

## Notes
- These colours align with the current Pagelayer/Elementor theme already installed
- Purple + Magenta is widely used in feminist and gender-rights organisations globally
- Amber adds warmth and an African/Kenyan visual identity
- All combinations pass WCAG AA contrast ratio for accessibility
